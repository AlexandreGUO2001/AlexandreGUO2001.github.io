\begin{rSection}{Research Summary and Interests}
I study diffusion-based generative models, with a current focus on continuous and discrete diffusion large language models and their post-training via distillation and reinforcement learning.

My broader work on sampling spans the theoretical analysis of sampling algorithms and the development of learning-based neural samplers.
\end{rSection}
